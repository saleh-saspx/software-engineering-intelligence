# Tax rules (agreed with our tax advisor, 2026-10)

All amounts are `Decimal`. Unless stated otherwise, tax is computed on the invoice subtotal and
rounded half-up to the cent once.

## EU (DE, FR, NL, AT)
- Standard and reduced rates: DE 19% (books 7%), FR 20% (books 5.5%), NL 21% (books 9%),
  AT 20% (books 10%).
- Reverse charge: a business customer with a VAT ID in a different country from the seller pays
  no VAT on our invoice.
- AT only: round the tax of each line half-up to the cent, then add the rounded line taxes.

## US
- Sales tax only for states where we have nexus: CA 7.25%, NY 4%, TX 6.25%. Other states: none.
- Groceries are exempt.

## Canada
- GST: 5% of the subtotal, rounded half-up to the cent (together with PST).
- PST by province: BC 7%, MB 7%, SK 6%; provinces not listed have no PST.
